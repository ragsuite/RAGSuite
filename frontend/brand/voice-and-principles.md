# RAGSuite — Voice & Principles
*Always-on. Read this before creating anything — web page, doc, deck, email, post, ad. It defines how RAGSuite sounds and the principles every output obeys.*
*Companion: `master-brand-guide.md` (how it looks). Locked facts: `positioning.md`, `../business-model/pricing.md`, `../product/ce-ee-matrix.md`.*

## Brand essence
**Sovereign, precise, calm.** RAGSuite is enterprise AI you stay in control of. We earn trust the way a German engineering firm does — with proof, predictability, and restraint, never hype.

## Personality
Confident · concrete · restrained · European · human-but-engineered. We're the steady expert in the room, not the loudest. An innovation by **NITSAN** — we carry that 15-year enterprise track record quietly.

## Voice attributes
1. **Concrete over clever.** Specifics, numbers, EUR figures, named features. If a sentence survives deleting its adjectives, it's on-voice.
2. **Calm authority.** Short declaratives. We state, we don't sell. No exclamation marks, no urgency theatre.
3. **Proof-led.** Every claim points to something inspectable — a citation, a spec, a policy, a price.
4. **Plain, not dumbed-down.** Respect a technical, senior reader. Explain mechanisms; skip the marketing fog.

## Tone by context
- **Website / marketing:** assured and editorial. Lead with control and predictability.
- **Sales:** consultative, honest about fit, transparent on price. Never pushy.
- **Documents / reports:** measured, structured, citeable. Let evidence carry it.
- **Email:** direct and human. One idea, one ask. Signed by a real person (default: Jürgen Pietschmann).
- **Social:** useful and understated. Share a fact or insight; no growth-hack tone, no emoji-spam.
- **Support / docs:** patient, exact, reassuring.
- **Legal (Impressum/Datenschutz):** formal, complete, German-correct.

## Writing principles
- Lead with **control & predictability**, not "AI magic."
- **"No hallucinations, just facts."** Citations are the trust story — reference them.
- **Open source = inspectability + no lock-in**, never "free."
- One predictable EUR invoice; **never** invent, round, discount, or convert prices.
- Name competitors only from `../market/` facts — never from memory.
- Flag honest watch-outs before decisions; offer options with trade-offs.

## Vocabulary
**Use:** sovereign, self-hosted, on your infrastructure, citation-backed, DSGVO by design, EU-AI-Act-ready, no phone-home, predictable, inspectable, Compare Models, quality loop, Community / Enterprise Edition, **AI Search, AI Assistant & AI Connectors** (the product triad — always all three; see `positioning.md`).
**Avoid / retired:** "revolutionary / magical / next-gen / game-changing"; "embeddable website AI assistant" as the category; TYPO3-based positioning for RAGSuite; "all-in-one AI app"; "no-code agent builder / automation workflow builder / agents / AI workspace / deep document understanding" as feature claims (deferred or off-positioning); "free" as the open-source pitch; n8n or the mobile app without the **Beta** label.

## German (DACH)
Formal **Sie** always. German for key pages/materials must be **native, not machine-translated** ("German words, not German trust"). Tech anglicisms are fine where natural (Open Source, Self-hosted, EU-AI-Act). Mind long compounds in layout (~30% expansion).

## Design principles (apply to every visual output)
1. **Every element earns its place.** Minimal is the rule, not a style.
2. **Editorial, not "app-marketing."** Type and whitespace carry the page; decoration doesn't.
3. **Proof over decoration.** Specs, citations, EUR figures.
4. **One signature, protected.** Sovereign-green accent + the citation motif are our fingerprint.
5. **Calm motion / calm layout.** Nothing performs; everything clarifies.

## Before you create anything — 6-point check
1. Does it lead with control/predictability (not hype)?
2. Are all facts from the KB (pricing, CE/EE, positioning, competitor claims)?
3. Beta labels on n8n + mobile? No retired/​deferred-feature wording?
4. Right tone for the medium and formal Sie if German?
5. On-brand visually (tokens, type, sovereign green, citation motif)?
6. Co-brand handled ("an innovation by NITSAN"), no TYPO3 positioning?
